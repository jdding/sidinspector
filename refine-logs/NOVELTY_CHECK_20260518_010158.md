## Closest Work And Overlap

### STAN / lifecycle-stage recommendation

STAN already makes lifecycle stage a recommender modeling variable and reports offline/online gains. Any idea that only adds lifecycle state embeddings, task routing, or MTL is not enough.

Differentiation required: lifecycle state must govern evidence validity or decision intervention, not just representation.

### Multi-source / multi-behavior recommendation

Many systems combine multiple behavior types, side information, sessions, or sources using attention/gating. Evidence Validity must avoid becoming generic source fusion.

Differentiation required: validity must be tied to deployment risk, source conflict, protected-hit cannibalization, or lifecycle-transition decisions.

### CASP / CALB internal prior

CASP already separates support expansion from deployable source-protected promotion. CALB/hybrid-source gates are durable partial evidence. Any new source arbitration paper risks being viewed as CASP v2.

Differentiation required: move from gate selection to validity estimation, uncertainty over cannibalization, intervention policy, or formal utility constraints.

### Churn-aware planning / online recommendation with churn

Return-session probing is close to decision-making under churn or abandonment risk. The public version is weak unless it can model intervention feedback credibly.

Differentiation required: focus on dormant-return evidence validity and source-protected early-session probing, ideally with production logging.

## Idea-Level Verdicts

### Idea 1: Evidence Validity Modeling for Lifecycle Transitions

**Novelty score**: 6/10.

**Why it survives first-pass screening**:

- It targets the strongest unresolved wiki bottleneck: support/candidate evidence does not equal deployable evidence.
- It is broader than CASP if it predicts evidence validity across sources and lifecycle transitions rather than selecting one promotion gate.
- It can absorb prior work as components: DeltaGate for temporal behavior collapse, CASP for protected deployment accounting, semantic bridges for auxiliary evidence.

**Killer objection**:

> This is just source gating with new terminology.

**Survival condition**:

Define an explicit validity target. Examples:

- probability that promoting a source recovers a hit without cannibalizing protected hits;
- state/source-level evidence calibration error;
- source-conflict risk under full-list intervention;
- validity certificate that decides when evidence is admissible for deployment.

Without one of these, abandon or merge into CASP extension.

### Idea 2: Transition-Aware Deployable Source Arbitration

**Novelty score**: 5/10.

**Why it is useful**:

- It cleanly generalizes CASP's support-to-deployment insight.
- It can produce a practical decision layer for lifecycle recommendation.

**Killer objection**:

> CASP already does constrained source promotion with gross/cannibal/net accounting. This is a relabeling unless the arbitration mechanism is new.

**Survival condition**:

Add a mechanism CASP does not have:

- calibrated cannibalization-risk prediction;
- uncertainty-aware source intervention;
- regret or robust-utility formulation;
- learned arbitration that preserves transparent constraints and beats CASP out of sample.

### Idea 3: Return-Session Preference Probing Under Protection Constraints

**Novelty score**: 6/10 public-only, 8/10 with production evidence.

**Why it is interesting**:

- It changes the problem from static re-ranking to early-session decision making.
- It aligns strongly with production lifecycle operations.

**Killer objection**:

> Public data cannot validate probing or abandonment risk; simulation may be arbitrary.

**Survival condition**:

Use production logs, randomized/gray-release exposure, or at minimum reconstructable session-level exposure sequences. If unavailable, keep this as a strategic future line rather than a public paper.

## Recommended Next Action

Proceed with Idea 1 only to a `research-refine` stage. The refinement must define the validity target before any experiment plan. If the target cannot be made distinct from CASP/CALB, do not launch experiments.

Keep Idea 3 as the production-aligned future path. It may become the stronger paper once internal logging and A/B evidence are available.
