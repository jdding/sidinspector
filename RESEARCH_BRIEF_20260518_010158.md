# Research Brief

## Working Title

Preference evidence validity for lifecycle-transition recommendation.

## Problem Statement

Dormant-return recommendation is no longer being treated as an isolated method niche. The new question is whether user lifecycle transitions create stale, sparse, and conflicting preference evidence that current recommenders cannot decide how to trust, protect, suppress, or probe.

The target is a method-level direction, not a protocol-only or public OPE paper.

## Context

The research wiki shows that the obvious dormant-return method paths have been heavily explored:

- Gap-duration adaptation is weak: GapMAML failed and rolling-window hybrid routing had no crossover point.
- Representation adapters are risky: LC-STC found candidate-level signal but failed full-catalog calibrated retrieval.
- CASP is the strongest positive line, but its lesson is support-to-deployment conversion under source protection, not another generic support expansion method.
- OPE remains valuable later, but public-only OPE novelty is too low for the next method mainline.

## Current Direction

Reframe the next search around:

> lifecycle transition decision under stale, sparse, and conflicting preference evidence.

Candidate evidence sources include old behavioral IDs, semantic/attribute bridges, current-session behavior, catalog trend signals, lifecycle state, and protected existing-source hits.

## Constraints

- Public phase only in this repo; no Huawei internal logs or business data.
- Use the research wiki as the primary prior and do not resurrect closed lines without a materially new mechanism.
- No experiments until the new ideas pass novelty and problem-fit screening.
- Avoid resource-only framing unless explicitly chosen as a fallback.

## Non-Goals

- Do not continue the public OPE preflight as the mainline.
- Do not make a generic lifecycle-conditioned SASRec/BERT4Rec paper.
- Do not restart gap-bucket, simple routing, LC-STC, LCP, or semantic-ID catalog-evolution variants.
- Do not claim online intervention validity without production logging/A-B evidence.

## Desired Output

A ranked idea-discovery report focused on:

1. preference evidence validity;
2. lifecycle transition decision policy;
3. deployable support conversion beyond CASP;
4. future production OPE only as a validation layer.
