## Why Gate 0 Remains Open

- Cluster A is not yet represented by a real TIGER/RQ-VAE/GRID-style SID export.
  `card_rqvae_feature_proxy` is a repaired compact feature-proxy, not the
  upstream CARD method and not a canonical Cluster A baseline.
- The Sports ReSID result is `resid_gaoq_unbalanced_proxy`, not the faithful
  balanced ReSID/GAOQ export. Its collision-free result is structurally induced
  by the unbalanced code assignment and must not be interpreted as ReSID
  codebook quality.
- CARD compact has pathological collision behavior in this run. It is useful as
  a controlled failure case, not as a method representative.
- The table mixes SID depths across methods, so D1/D2/D5a comparisons need
  normalization or explicit caveats before paper use.
- The displayed "D3" is category-purity proxy, not the original
  semantic-collaborative alignment diagnostic.
- The summary table does not expose the D4 head-tail or D5a trie-cost outputs,
  even if those CSVs exist in the metric directories.
- The dataset pivot from `Musical_Instruments` to `Sports_and_Outdoors` is a
  good reviewer-facing direction, but it must be recorded as a scope change
  rather than treated as implicit continuity from the original primary dataset.
